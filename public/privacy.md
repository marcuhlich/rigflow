## 2. What data we process

### Account data

When creating a user account, we process:

- Name
- Email address
- Password (stored exclusively encrypted/hashed, never in plain text)

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (performance of a contract)

### Content data

All data you actively enter into Rigflow – inventory items, projects, lending processes, DMX patch plans, and similar content – is processed solely to provide the core functionality of the app and is not used for any other purpose.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR

### Usage data / analytics

With your consent, we process anonymized usage data to improve the app. Without consent, no analytics data is collected.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent)

---

## 3. App permissions

### Camera

The camera is used exclusively to scan QR codes to identify inventory items. No photos are stored or transmitted. Access is only active during scanning.

### Push notifications

Rigflow can send optional push notifications (e.g. lending deadlines or project events). Consent is given via the system prompt and can be revoked anytime in system settings.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR (consent)

### File access / upload

Storage access is required to upload files (e.g. images or documents). Files are only transmitted when you explicitly trigger an upload.

---

## 4. Consent to data analysis

On first launch, you can choose whether anonymized usage data may be collected. This is optional and does not affect functionality.

You can withdraw consent anytime in the app settings.

## 6. Storage & deletion

Account and content data are stored while your account is active. After deletion, all data is removed within 30 days.

Analytics data is deleted according to provider retention policies. Individual deletion is not possible due to anonymization.
